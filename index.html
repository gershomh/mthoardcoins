Questions

Where can I buy Mountain Hoard Coins packs?

Live on Whatnot at @mhcoins.

How do I know what's in a series?

Every series has a complete checklist on this page showing each coin, its year, weight, and grade or condition.

Can a series change after it's released?

No. Once a series is marked finalized, its pack count and contents are locked.

How are ungraded coins described?

Raw coins are placed in one of three condition groups: Poor–Fair, Good–Extremely Fine, or Uncirculated–Mint.
